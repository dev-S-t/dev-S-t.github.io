// How I work

From the real problem to the thing that ships.

  1. 01

    Find the real problem

    Before a single screen: who is stuck, where, and why it matters to the business.

  2. 02

    Sketch the first idea

    Rough lines first, many of them, while changing your mind is still free.

  3. 03

    Build it

    The design becomes the real thing, in Framer, WordPress or code, by the same hands.

  4. 04

    Own it until it ships

    QA, the handoff, launch day and the fixes after it. Nothing is thrown over a wall.
