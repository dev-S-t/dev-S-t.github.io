System Overview & Paper Reference

Software simulation and predictive model platform published in IEEE: A Demand-Driven Software Approach with Dynamic Micro-Expiry and Just-in-Time Processing to Reduce Platelet Wastage in Blood Banks.

Empirical Results

  • Platelet Wastage: Reduced from 11.2% down to 2.5% (~78% relative reduction).
  • Demand Fulfillment: Maintained 99.1% supply fulfillment.
  • Model Accuracy: SARIMA achieved the lowest Mean Absolute Error (MAE: 5.85).
  • Statistical Significance: Validated over 30 simulation iterations using paired t-tests (p < 1.22×10⁻¹²).
