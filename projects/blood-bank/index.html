Blood Bank Demand-Forecasting System

Engineering Specification — Sahil Tomar (dev-S-t)

Back to Projects Hub | View IEEE Publication Page

IEEE Research & Simulation Model

Demand-driven blood bank supply optimization combining SARIMA/XGBoost time-series forecasting with dynamic micro-expiry logic.

  • Platelet Wastage: Reduced from 11.2% down to 2.5% (~78% relative reduction).
  • Fulfillment Rate: Maintained 99.1% patient demand fulfillment.
  • Statistical Validation: Validated over 30 Monte Carlo simulation runs (p < 1.22×10⁻¹²).
