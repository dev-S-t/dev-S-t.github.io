System Overview & Topology Inversion

Designed for compliance and legal clients who refuse to upload documents to external services. Instead of shipping documents out to a cloud pipeline, the RAG topology is inverted: reasoning agents deploy directly onto the client's local infrastructure.

Engineering Highlights

  • Agent-to-Agent (A2A) Protocol: Built over A2A protocol, allowing external agents or human interfaces to query local knowledge without underlying raw documents ever leaving the client's network.
  • Hybrid Dense + Keyword Search: Combines dense semantic vector search with keyword indexing, preventing retrieval degradation across mixed formats (contracts, spreadsheets, source code, scanned PDFs).
