IEEE Research Paper

A Demand-Driven Software Approach with Dynamic Micro-Expiry and Just-in-Time Processing to Reduce Platelet Wastage in Blood Banks

Publisher: IEEE | Document ID: IEEE Xplore #11584332

Authors: Sahil Tomar (Co-Author & Lead Software Simulation Modeler)

Abstract & Key Contribution

Platelet concentrates have a short shelf life of 5 days, resulting in significant wastage in blood banks due to inventory mismatch. This paper presents a software framework combining time-series forecasting (SARIMA, XGBoost, Prophet, ARIMA) with dynamic micro-expiry logic and Just-in-Time (JIT) preparation routines.

Empirical Results & Statistical Validation

  • Wastage Reduction: Reduced simulated platelet wastage from 11.2% to 2.5% (~78% relative reduction).
  • Fulfillment Rate: Maintained a 99.1% patient demand fulfillment rate under peak demand conditions.
  • Forecasting Accuracy: SARIMA model achieved the lowest Mean Absolute Error (MAE = 5.85) among tested algorithms.
  • Statistical Significance: Validated across 30 Monte Carlo simulation runs using paired t-tests (p < 1.22×10⁻¹²).
