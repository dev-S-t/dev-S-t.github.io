05 Process

How it goes

  1. 01

    Listen

    We start with your problem, not our tools.

    Mostly nodding. Some note-taking.
  2. 02

    Sketch

    Rough lines first, so ideas stay cheap to change.

    Where the whiteboard gets messy.
  3. 03

    Build

    Design and AI built side by side, shipped in small pieces.

    Shipped. Celebrated. Fixed one more thing.
  4. 04

    Stay in the loop

    We keep watching after launch and tune what needs tuning.

    We don't ghost. It's in the name.
